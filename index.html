Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between full and partial dentures?

Full dentures replace all of the teeth in an upper or lower arch. Partial dentures fill the gaps when some natural teeth remain, and they usually attach to those teeth.


How long does it take to get new dentures?

It depends on the type and on whether teeth need to be removed first. We explain the timeline at your consultation, including the number of visits for impressions, try-ins and the final fitting.


Will my dentures need adjustments?

Yes, that is normal. Gums change over time, and small adjustments after fitting help with comfort. We schedule follow-ups to keep the fit right.


What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, check and all major credit cards. We explain the cost of your treatment before it begins.

Denture Services in Ozone Park, NY

Here is a closer look at the denture services we provide, from full and partial dentures to custom design and fittings.

Full Dentures

Full dentures replace every tooth in the upper or lower arch, or both, and are designed to restore chewing, speech and appearance. We take accurate impressions, record how your jaws meet and use try-ins to check the shape, color and position of the teeth before the final denture is made. A well-made full denture rests comfortably on the gums and looks natural. We also explain how to clean it, how to adapt to eating with it and what to expect during the first weeks.

Partial Dentures

When some of your natural teeth remain, a partial denture can fill the gaps and prevent the other teeth from drifting out of position. It consists of replacement teeth on a gum-colored base, held in place by clasps or precision attachments that fit around existing teeth. We design the framework so that it is comfortable and as discreet as possible, and we explain how to insert, remove and clean it. A partial denture is often a practical way to restore function without replacing every tooth.

Custom Full and Partial Dentures

Every mouth is different, and so a denture should be too. Custom design starts with a conversation about what you want: natural-looking shapes, a particular shade or teeth that match the ones you have. We use your measurements, facial features and bite to guide tooth selection and arrangement, and we adjust the try-in until it looks right to you. The result is a denture made for your face and your mouth, rather than one chosen from a standard set.

Denture Fitting and Adjustments

A good fit makes the difference between a denture you forget about and one that rubs and slips. At fitting, we check how the denture sits on the gums, how your teeth meet and whether speech feels natural. We make fine adjustments to relieve sore spots and balance the bite, then schedule follow-up visits as you get used to the new teeth. Gums and bone change over time, so we also help with later adjustments to keep your dentures comfortable.
